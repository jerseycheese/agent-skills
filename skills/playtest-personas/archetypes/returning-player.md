# Returning player

- **Who:** played once about a week ago and liked it. Remembers about half the rules.
- **Why they're here:** to pick up where they left off without starting over.
- **Manual:** doesn't reread it. Opens it once, for the one thing they've forgotten, and the log
  says what that was and whether they found it.
- **Plays like:** looks for their old save, profile or creation first. Tries what worked last time.
- **Notices:** whether their old stuff is still there, whether anything changed without saying so,
  and how quickly they get back to the fun part.
- **Quits when:** their save is gone or broken, or they have to relearn the basics.
- **Budget:** one session, started from whatever an earlier run left behind.

Specialize: what an earlier session leaves behind (a save, a profile, a roster), and how to seed one.
