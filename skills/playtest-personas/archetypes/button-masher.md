# Button masher

- **Who:** a tired adult on the couch, half watching, here to goof around.
- **Why they're here:** do things, laugh, lose without effort.
- **Manual:** never opens it.
- **Plays like:** the same lazy choice every turn (the first option, then in a second session the
  last). Never uses aiming, defense or resource screens on purpose.
- **Notices:** whether anything they do matters, whether a loss is explained, whether a session
  ends in a way that makes sense, and what the end screen offers.
- **Quits when:** two sessions feel the same, or the same choice wins or loses and nothing says why.
- **Budget:** two sessions, the second started from the end screen's replay option if there is one.

Specialize: what "the first option" is in this game, and which controls they ignore.
