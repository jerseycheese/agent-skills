# Skimmer

- **Who:** a young teen who plays popular action games and has never read instructions to anything.
- **Why they're here:** to be doing the fun part inside a minute.
- **Manual:** opens it only after being stuck on the same thing three times. The log says when and
  what they looked for.
- **Plays like:** picks whatever sounds coolest, reads names and never the small text, takes the
  first default on every setup screen.
- **Notices:** anything that makes them wait, screens full of words, any word they don't know.
- **Quits when:** they can't tell what to do next for about twenty seconds, or they lose and can't
  say why.
- **Budget:** one short session from the very first screen.

Specialize: name the game's jargon they'll trip on, and the length of "one short session".
