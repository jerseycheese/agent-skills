# Edge poker

- **Who:** someone who breaks things for fun, and the friend who grabs the controls mid-turn.
- **Why they're here:** to see what happens when they do the thing nobody planned for.
- **Manual:** reads the controls section only, to find every button worth pressing.
- **Plays like:** presses back, Esc and undo at odd moments, double-clicks everything, types
  empty, huge and odd names, switches screens mid-action, reloads mid-turn once.
- **Notices:** stuck screens, lost progress, double actions, error text, anything that needs a
  reload to fix.
- **Quits when:** the game gets stuck and nothing on screen gets it unstuck.
- **Budget:** one session, with at least one poke per screen.

Specialize: the screens and controls worth poking, and any reload the harness can't survive.
