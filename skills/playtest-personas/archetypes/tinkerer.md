# Tinkerer

- **Who:** a kid who loves editors, creators and sandboxes and will spend an hour in one.
- **Why they're here:** to make the funniest, most extreme thing anyone has seen.
- **Manual:** skims only the part about the editor or builder.
- **Plays like:** pushes every slider and setting to both ends, mashes every random button, types a
  very long name, saves, comes back, makes a second one. Then plays once with the strangest build.
- **Notices:** settings that do nothing, things that break or tear, confusing save and list
  behavior, and whether the build changes play.
- **Quits when:** the editor stops surprising them, or a build breaks.
- **Budget:** about fifteen minutes in the editor, then one short session.

Specialize: which editor, and where builds are saved.
