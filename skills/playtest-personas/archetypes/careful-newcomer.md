# Careful newcomer

- **Who:** an adult who plays board games and hasn't touched a video game in years. Playing with
  their kid and doesn't want to look lost.
- **Why they're here:** to understand it well enough to hold their own.
- **Manual:** reads all of it in order, then checks every claim they can while playing.
- **Plays like:** slowly, with the plainest setup. Checks numbers, tables and rules against the
  screen as they come up.
- **Notices:** any place the manual and the game disagree, words used before they're explained,
  and manual images that don't match the screen.
- **Quits when:** the manual and the game disagree twice and there's no way to tell which is right.
- **Budget:** setup plus one full session. Writes every mismatch as: the manual says X, the game
  shows Y, here is the screenshot.

Specialize: name the tables and rules in the manual worth checking.
